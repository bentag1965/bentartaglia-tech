AI Council Experiment

A challenge should survive its answer.

The Oracle Table puts seven AI personas into a continuing conversation and preserves the objections they raise. The experiment can move forward without pretending every disagreement has been settled.

The question behind the mechanism

Can disagreement remain accountable without compulsory agreement?

Preserve the challenge

Each recorded objection retains its source turn, target persona, and exact evidence passage. The record is append-only.

Track the response

Responses may answer, revise, concede, dispute, decline, avoid, or remain uncertain. A response does not automatically close the objection.

Keep the history

Explicit editorial state changes distinguish open, deferred, disputed, resolved, and withdrawn challenges. Earlier evidence stays visible.

Private validation

Operational progress, with limits.

The validation completed 24 turns, 24 assessments, and 24 reviews. It recorded 29 new objections and 33 response events, including responses to older challenges. One generation attempt was rejected before a later attempt succeeded. No ledger-validation errors were recorded in the completed reviews.

Those counts show that the mechanism ran. They do not establish extraction accuracy, a resolution rate, consciousness, or a general improvement in reasoning. Sampled exchanges included concessions, revisions, and continuing disputes. The private transcript remains private.

The numeric evaluation rubric was retained, but the evaluator’s measurement packet changed. Comparisons with earlier episodes need that qualification. Further review should examine mistaken attribution, missed objections, and drift across repeated runs.

Publication and audio

The conversation travels with context.

TGO hosts the episode editions and editorial summaries. Social snippets retain the speaker, episode link, and the fact that this is generated dialogue. Claims made by personas are provisional and may be wrong; they are not endorsed facts or statements of my beliefs.

Podcast adaptations begin with a script: an opening, selected exchanges, the objections that remain, and a closing that acknowledges uncertainty. No audio episode is available yet.
