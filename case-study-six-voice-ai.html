AI Systems · Project record updated November 9, 2026

Six voices at the table. One question repeated over time.

This work has two connected tracks: orchestrated conversations between six AI personas, and a longitudinal study that returns to a frozen question at scheduled intervals. They answer different questions, so their evidence is kept separate.

Track 1 · The Oracle Table

A moderated conversation, with the rough edges left visible.

Six recurring AI personas take turns in a directed conversation. A separate director role manages topic and speaker selection. The goal is to study how disagreements, objections, and distinctions develop across a conversation—not to present the models as independent experts or as a consensus engine.

Completed public milestone: Episode 8 began on October 18, 2026 and preserved 19 Council turns. Provider refusal and generation errors stopped the next turn; the interruption was acknowledged and an editorial close was published with the episode on October 23, 2026.

Track 2 · Repeated-Question Study

Same question, new sessions, scheduled checkpoints.

Within each weekly cohort, the study freezes a question and asks it again at day 0, 1, 7, 14, 21, 30, 90, 180, and 365. Each checkpoint uses four fresh sessions at short offsets. The design is meant to separate short-window response variation from changes observed over longer periods.

Preserve the conditions

Record the prompt version, model and provider version, sampling window, and response so later comparisons have context.

Keep evaluation separate

Store generated responses independently from automated scoring and human review. A score is not a verified explanation for why an answer changed.

Publish only reviewed evidence

Mark partial runs and missing observations clearly. Do not turn incomplete cohorts into provider rankings or claims about drift.
