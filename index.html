How I Design Operational Systems

Different technologies. The same operating discipline.

Observe what is happening, establish state, control risk, handle failure, preserve evidence, recover, and improve. I apply that discipline to infrastructure, identity, connected devices, automation, and AI.

Observable

Know what the system is actually doing. Make health, exceptions, and missing evidence visible.

Stateful

Preserve enough context to know what happened and resume work safely.

Resilient

Assume networks, APIs, devices, and people will fail. Define degraded operation and recovery before they do.

Governed

Let authority and consequence determine autonomy. Make ownership, approval, and stop conditions explicit.

Auditable

Important actions should leave evidence: inputs, versions, decisions, approvals, and outcomes.

Supportable

Someone other than the builder must be able to deploy, troubleshoot, replace, and operate the system.
