Why the Lab Exists

Current capability should be visible, not implied.

A résumé can show where someone has worked. It is much weaker at showing how they think today.

The Lab exists to make architecture judgment, technical depth, operational reasoning, documentation habits, and current hands-on development visible without exposing employer or customer systems.

Portfolio Boundary

Public enough to inspect. Sanitized enough to be responsible.

The public repositories are reference implementations and generalized tools. They do not contain former-employer source code, customer data, credentials, private tenant information, proprietary device configurations, internal network details, or production secrets.
